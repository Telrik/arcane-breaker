A QUICK INITIATION

Master the tower.

Move & launch
Drag anywhere in the arena, or use A / D and the arrow keys. Tap the arena or press Space to launch.
Cast a spell
Tap a spell button, or use Q / W / E / R / T / Y. Mana regenerates while you play. Fireball is the only attack that damages titanium.
Stay alive
A lost orb costs 14 vitality. Force Field prevents this damage for 9 seconds; Heal restores 26 vitality. Launch another orb to continue.
Take a break
Use Pause, P, or Escape. Switching tabs pauses the game automatically. Resume when you're ready.
